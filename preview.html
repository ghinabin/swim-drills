Recovery + technique

POOL TIME75-90 min
EQUIPMENT
KickboardPull buoy
1
Warm-up
300 m: 100 m easy freestyle + 50 m backstroke + 50 m easy with pull buoy + 100 m gradually faster.
2
Technique
6 x 25 m relaxed freestyle. Keep the body long and hips high. Rest 20-30 sec.
3
Dolphin + breakout
6 reps from the wall. No kickboard. Tight streamline -> 2-4 quick dolphin kicks -> smooth breakout -> 4-6 strong strokes. Rest 45-60 sec.
4
Turn practice
6 flip turns. Start 5-7 m before the wall -> attack the wall -> no glide -> quick flip -> hard push -> 2-4 dolphin kicks -> breakout. Rest 45-60 sec.
5
Easy aerobic
6 x 50 m at 65-70%. On reps 3 and 6 use the pull buoy and swim easy. Rest 25-30 sec.
6
Speed finish
4 x 25 m: first half easy, second half fast. Rest 60 sec.
7
Cooldown
200 m very easy.
RECORD TODAY
Fatigue 1-10 • Which part felt hardest? • Any pain: yes/no
If you have pain, unusual dizziness, illness, or technique becomes unsafe, stop the hard set and recover.
