## How to test

On **September 18**, confirm the baseline with one push-start 50 using your usual turn, then do one permitted deck-dive 50. On **September 25**, do two deck-dive 50s; on **October 2**, one. Rest at least five minutes between tests and longer if needed. Easy recovery lengths are already included in the session.

If you still feel fatigued or your warm-up builds are unusually slow, replace both timed 50s with easy 50s and keep the whole session easy. Each rehearsal joins start → dolphin kicks → breakout → first 25 → turn → dolphin kicks → breakout → return 25 → finish touch. Use your reliable side turn until a flip turn is consistently quicker. Practise flips separately on Monday.

Record date, course, start type, turn type, time and one execution observation in a training log. An observer can time the 25 m split or operate the app stopwatch. Deck, push and block starts are separate comparisons; changing the start alone is not evidence of a fitness gain. Use the same timing method. Saturday's scheduled 50 m pool cannot provide a turn within a 50 m swim. Weekday deck rehearsals practise the whole swim, while Saturday practises the block launch. Confirm the actual meet course.

## What supports the goal—and what needed changing

- **Keep:** fist/open-hand contrast, controlled pull-buoy work, kicking, turns, starts and breakouts. Transfer the drill's cue into normal freestyle. Their value for you must show up in execution or measured swimming; we cannot prove each drill's benefit from the schedule alone.
- **Change:** three consecutive hard days and a supposedly light Sunday containing maximum swimming. Easy days and full rest now have clear roles. If repeated efforts slow or technique deteriorates, extend recovery; if that fails, stop the hard set. Skip a test when unusually fatigued. Do not make up missed sprints.
- **Individualise:** dolphin-kick count and breakout distance. More kicks can be slower. Time through the breakout to 15 m, including surface swimming. Breathe as needed; no forced zero-breath lengths or hyperventilation. Practise dives and underwaters with direct supervision and only where permitted.
- **Endurance:** Tuesday supports aerobic fitness; Friday practises sustaining race speed across 50 m. This is a short 50 m preparation block, not a complete endurance programme for longer events. Do not suddenly add large mileage or unfamiliar gym work close to the meet.
