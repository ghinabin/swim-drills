# Breaststroke-only plan — speed, endurance, dive and turns

**29 September–12 October 2026 · Target: 12 October, 50 m breaststroke, 25 m pool, 14th NSA Championship, Satdobato · Rest day: Saturday only**

All water work is breaststroke apart from the explicitly optional backstroke recovery lengths. Confirm entries, start rules, and warm-up-pool access with your coach.

## How to use the plan

- Pool window: 07:30–09:30. Finish when the listed work is done.
- Effort scale: easy 2–4/10; controlled 5–7/10; quick 8/10; race pace 9/10; max 10/10 only where prescribed with full recovery.
- Rest is after each repetition, not a send-off. Start again only when breathing has settled.
- On an amber day—heavy legs, repeated gasping, broken timing, or lingering fatigue—replace fast work with easy 25s and move on to the next dated session. Never make up missed metres.
- If easy 50s cause marked breathlessness, split them into 2 × 25 m with 20–30 seconds extra rest. Stop for unusual breathlessness, wheezing, chest discomfort, or dizziness.
- Stop breaststroke kick work if your knees or groin hurt. Do not train through pain.
- No breath-hold sets, hyperventilation, underwater time/distance targets, or forced kick counts. All swimming and starts are supervised.

## Breaststroke technique card

| Focus | What to practise |
|---|---|
| Stroke timing | Pull and breathe, recover the hands forward, then kick into a long body line. Use a brief glide when easy and a faster connected rhythm at race effort. |
| Kick | Turn the feet outward through the propulsive phase. Keep the kick comfortable and coordinated; never force a wide knee position. |
| Distance per stroke | Count strokes per 25 m. Seek fewer strokes at the same or better time, not more effort. |
| Stroke rate | Use a faster connected turnover at race effort—shorter strokes taken quickly, not long strokes rushed. |
| Dive and pullout | Enter flat and shallow in a locked streamline. Complete one legal arm pull, one optional legal dolphin kick, then breaststroke kick. Break out head first and surface sooner whenever needed. |
| Turn | Touch with both hands together and separated. Release one hand immediately, tuck quickly, plant both feet, push straight back, then complete the legal pullout. |
| Finish | Keep tempo to the wall and make a simultaneous, separated two-hand touch at speed rather than gliding in. |
